# Bibliography Screening and Use Candidates

Use this reference after the paper has been analyzed.

## Bibliography Screening

Extract sources worth following only when they do at least one of these:

- define or clarify a central concept;
- support or attack a premise;
- provide an important objection;
- supply a vivid case;
- mark a historical turning point;
- represent a major position;
- provide recent or foundational intervention;
- clarify the paper's dialectical target or debate-framing profile.

Do not cite or queue sources merely because they appear in the bibliography.

Use two groups:

```markdown
### Verified Sources Worth Using

- APA reference if verified. Role:

### Follow-Up Leads Requiring Verification

- Author (Year?), probable title. Why it may matter. Verification needed:
```
